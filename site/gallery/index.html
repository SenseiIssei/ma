Community gallery

Something worth learning.

Decks written by people who use Ma. Each one teaches first and practises after, with examples and short notes. Open a deck in Ma on your iPhone, or download the JSON and import it yourself.

"Open in Ma" works on an iPhone with Ma installed. On other devices, download the JSON and send it to your phone.

Contribute a deck

Share what you know.

Every deck in this gallery is a JSON file in the gallery/decks folder. To add yours:

  1. Fork the repository and add gallery/decks/<id>.en.json or <id>.de.json. Editions of one deck share the id and the card ids.
  2. Give it a title, a subtitle of at most 60 characters, a one-character symbol, a category (languages, knowledge, mind, tech or life) and a locale.
  3. Write at least ten cards with ids <id>-001, <id>-002 and so on. Answers stay at 30 characters or less, each card has three plausible wrong answers, an example sentence that contains the answer word for word, and a short note that explains why.
  4. Run python gallery/build_index.py. It checks every rule and rewrites index.json.
  5. Open a pull request. Facts get checked before a deck goes live.

Please keep it factual and kind: no advertising, no medical or financial advice dressed up as fact, and only content you have the right to share.
