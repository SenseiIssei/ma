## Description (4000)

### English

Ma is the space between two things. This app puts that space between your thumb and the next feed.

Pick the apps that pull at you. When you open one, Ma steps in: a calm screen, one breath, and a question from a topic you actually want to learn. Answer it and the app opens for a few minutes. Or let it be.

BOUNDARIES THAT HOLD
- Block apps, categories and websites with Apple's Screen Time
- Time windows and templates: morning calm, deep work, night
- Lockdown: everything closed for up to a night, no way through
- Daily unlock limits and rising friction: every unlock today costs one more question
- Open without Reels: jump to the website in Safari, where Ma Filter hides Reels, Shorts and endless feeds

LEARN FIRST, THEN PRACTISE
- Every new card is explained before you are asked about it
- Eight exercise types: choice, true or not, fill the gap, match the pairs, build the sentence, type it, and more
- More than 20 topics in English and German: languages (English, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, Japanese), general knowledge, history, science, psychology, Python, Git, first aid, mental math
- Make your own topics, paste lists, import JSON, or let the on-device model create one

BETTER DAYS
- Focus timer with calm sounds that keep playing when the screen is locked
- Morning check-in and evening reflection
- Small daily habits with streaks
- Breathing exercises: box, 4-7-8, calm
- Balance: short guided workouts, water and meal check, a wind-down before bed
- Widgets and a focus timer in the Dynamic Island

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your Screen Time choices stay on your iPhone, Apple only gives Ma anonymous placeholders. Friends circles are optional and share only numbers, never content.

Ma is free and open source: github.com/SenseiIssei/ma

## App Privacy (nutrition label)

Data Not Collected. Friends circles (optional) store a random id, a nickname and daily numbers on the developer's server, not linked to identity; declare "Other usage data, not linked to you, app functionality" if circles ship in the reviewed build.

## Review notes

Ma uses the Family Controls framework with individual authorization so a person can limit their own apps. Screen Time permission is requested in onboarding. To test the gate: create a boundary under Boundaries, pick any app, open it, tap "Answer a question" on the shield and open the notification. The Safari extension "Ma Filter" is optional and only hides elements on social sites.
