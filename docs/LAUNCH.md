**Description:**
Ma is a free, open-source iPhone app that puts a pause between you and social media. Open Instagram and Ma steps in: one breath, one question from a topic you want to learn, then you decide. It blocks with Apple's Screen Time, teaches before it tests, and adds the small things that make a day better: a focus timer with calm sounds, habits, breathing, short workouts and a wind-down at night. No account, no ads, no tracking.

## Reddit

**r/nosurf, r/digitalminimalism**

Title: I made a free app that asks me a learning question every time I open Instagram

Body:
Blockers never worked for me, I just switched them off. So I built one that doesn't say no. It says "one breath and one question first". The questions come from topics I actually want to learn (Japanese, history, first aid), and new cards are explained before they are asked, so it doesn't feel like a test.

A few things I added because I needed them:
- "Open without Reels": it offers the Instagram website in Safari, where a filter hides Reels and Shorts
- a lockdown with no way out for evenings
- every extra unlock in a day costs one more question

It's free and open source, no account, nothing leaves the phone. Would love feedback from people here: github.com/SenseiIssei/ma
