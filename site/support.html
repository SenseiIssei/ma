Questions and answers.

The things people ask most. If your question is not here, open an issue on GitHub and describe what you expected and what happened.

Blocking

Why can't Ma hide Reels inside the Instagram app?

Because iOS does not allow any app to reach into another one. Every app runs in its own sandbox, and no App Store app, Ma included, can change what the Instagram, YouTube or TikTok app shows.

What does work is a combination:

  1. Put the Instagram app inside a boundary in Ma, so opening it asks a question first or is not possible at all.
  2. Use instagram.com in Safari with Ma Filter switched on. The filter hides Reels there, and messages, profiles and posts keep working.

The same works for YouTube Shorts on youtube.com. Shorts links open as normal videos.

What is the Shortcuts mode, and when do I need it?

The Shortcuts mode puts Ma's pause in front of your apps without Screen Time. An automation in Apple's Shortcuts app starts Ma whenever one of your chosen apps opens. If you have an open pass, Ma stays invisible and the app opens as usual.

You need it while a build of Ma cannot use Screen Time yet (Apple has to approve Family Controls for every app), or if you simply prefer it. Set it up once:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and go to the Automation tab.
  2. Tap +, then choose "App".
  3. Tap "Choose" and tick every app that pulls at you. One automation covers all of them.
  4. Leave "Is Opened" ticked and choose "Run Immediately". Switch "Notify When Run" off.
  5. Tap Next, search for "Ma Pause" and pick it. Done.

Apple does not let any app create automations on its own, so this one step is yours. In Ma, Boundaries shows whether the automation has run.

Why does the blocked screen send a notification instead of opening Ma?

The screen iOS shows over a blocked app is not allowed to open another app. So "Answer a question" sends a notification, and tapping it takes you into Ma: one breath, the question, then your decision.

I really need a blocked app right now. What can I do?

Answer the question and open it: that is what the gate is for. A boundary set to "no way through" and a running lockdown have no unlock button; a lockdown ends early with five right answers.

And there is always the last resort: revoking Ma's Screen Time permission in the iOS Settings app lifts every boundary at once. That is on purpose. Letting go should always be possible.

Safari filter

How do I switch on the Safari filter?
  1. Open the Settings app, then Apps, Safari, Extensions.
  2. Tap Ma Filter and switch it on.
  3. Allow it for instagram.com, youtube.com, x.com, linkedin.com, facebook.com and tiktok.com. "Allow" for those sites is enough; it does not need every website.
  4. Reload any open tabs of those sites.

You can also manage it straight from Safari: tap the page menu next to the address bar and choose Manage Extensions. What exactly the filter hides is set in Ma under Boundaries.

I still see Reels or Shorts in Safari.

Check that the filter is allowed for that exact site, then reload the page. If it still shows up, the site has probably changed its layout, which big sites do often. Please open an issue with the site and what you see, and the filter gets updated.

Decks and learning

How do I import a deck?

There are four ways:

  • Community gallery in the app. In Ma, open Learn, tap + next to Topics and choose the community gallery. Pick a deck, look at the preview and tap Add.
  • From this website. Open the deck gallery on your iPhone and tap "Open in Ma". Ma downloads the deck and adds it.
  • A JSON file. Save the file to Files, then in Ma tap + next to Topics and choose "Import JSON file". One file can hold one deck or a list of decks.
  • From the clipboard. Copy deck JSON, for example from an AI chat, and choose "JSON from clipboard".

An import never overwrites anything: if a deck with the same id exists, the new one gets its own id. Imported decks can be edited and deleted like your own.

How do I make my own deck?

In Ma, tap + next to Topics and choose "New topic", then type or paste your cards. Pasting whole lists works: one card per line, question and answer separated by a tab, ;, | or = .

For bigger decks, "Copy AI template" puts a prompt on your clipboard. Paste it into a chat with any AI, fill in your topic, and import the answer with "JSON from clipboard". The format itself is simple:

{"title": "Korean", "cards": [{"prompt": "물", "answer": "water", "distractors": ["fire", "tree", "rice"], "example": "I drink water.", "note": "mul"}]}

Only prompt and answer are required. An example that contains the answer word for word unlocks fill-the-gap exercises.

Can I add my deck to the community gallery?

Yes, please. Decks live in the gallery/decks folder of the repository. Add your file as <id>.en.json or <id>.de.json, run python gallery/build_index.py to check it and refresh the index, and open a pull request. The gallery page lists the rules.

Why does the gate never ask me new cards?

On purpose. Nobody should be quizzed on something they were never shown. New cards are taught first in a lesson (at most three at a time, practised right away), and only cards you have learned show up before an app.

General

Which languages does Ma speak?

English and German. Ma follows your iPhone's language. To use a different one for Ma only, open Settings, Apps, Ma, Language. Bundled decks switch edition with the app language and keep your progress.

What does Ma cost?

Nothing. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription. Ma is open source under the MIT licence.

When will Ma be on the App Store?

As soon as Apple has approved the Family Controls entitlement that Screen Time apps need, and the app has passed review. Until then, the code is on GitHub for anyone who wants to build it.

Where do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

On GitHub issues. Mention your iPhone model, the iOS version and what you did right before it happened. Please leave out personal data, issues are public.
