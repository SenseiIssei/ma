What Ma does

Six quiet tools, one calm app.

Ma is not about willpower. It adds a little space where the pull is strongest, and gives that space something worth doing.

Boundaries

Block what pulls at you

Block lists of apps, categories and websites through Apple's Screen Time, each with its own time window, number of questions and unlock length.

  • Templates for social media, morning calm, deep work and night
  • Daily unlock limits, rising friction, a short wait
  • Lockdown: everything off until the next morning
Learn first, then practise

A question at the gate

Before a blocked app opens, Ma asks about a topic you chose. New cards are taught before they are asked, so the gate only asks what you have already learned.

  • Eight exercise types, from multiple choice to typing
  • Spaced repetition with a Leitner box per card
  • Bundled decks, your own, or the community gallery
Focus

A timer that means it

Pomodoro with 25, 5 and 15 minutes, all adjustable. During a round every boundary sleeps, without exceptions if you want, and the timer keeps running when Ma is closed.

  • Rain, brown noise, ocean and a night drone, generated on the device
  • A jump into Spotify search if you prefer your own music
Today

Breathing, habits, check-ins

Three rings for the day: focus, learning and habits. Small rituals that take a minute and add up.

  • Morning check-in with mood and one intention
  • Evening reflection with three short questions
  • Box, 4-7-8 and calm breathing with haptics
  • Daily habits with streaks
Balance

Movement, food and sleep

The body side of a calmer day, kept deliberately simple. No calorie counting, no scores to chase.

  • Guided routines: morning, desk, seven minutes, evening
  • Water and meals with a few honest tags
  • A bedtime, a wake time and a wind-down before sleep
Safari filter

The web without Reels

Ma Filter is a Safari extension that hides the endless parts of social sites and leaves the useful parts alone.

  • Hides Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X trends, the LinkedIn feed and Facebook Reels
  • Opens Shorts links as normal videos
How the gate works

Open Instagram. Land somewhere calm.

  1. You reach for an app

    Instagram, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, TikTok: whatever sits inside one of your boundaries.

  2. A calm screen first

    Instead of the feed you see a quiet shield. One tap sends you into Ma.

  3. One breath, one question

    Answer a card you have already learned. You get a few minutes of something useful out of every urge.

  4. Then you decide

    "Open" and "I'll leave it" are the same size. Letting it be counts too.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone.

There is no Ma server to send anything to. Your rules, answers, habits and moods live in the app's own storage on the device.

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers
  • Screen Time data never leaves the device
  • Optional AI features run on the device only

Read the privacy policy

Honest limits

What Ma cannot do.

iOS draws some firm lines, and they are there for good reasons. Better you hear it here than find out later.

Reels inside the Instagram app stay

No iOS app may reach into another one. Ma can block the Instagram app, and the Safari filter hides Reels on instagram.com. Together that gives you Instagram without Reels.

You can always switch it off

Revoking the Screen Time permission in iOS Settings lifts every boundary. That is on purpose: letting go should always be possible.

Screen Time needs Apple's approval

Apple reviews every app that uses Family Controls. Until the approval is through, the Shortcuts mode puts the pause in front of your apps instead.

Fifty apps per boundary

Screen Time allows at most 50 individual apps in one selection. For more, pick a whole category or split them across boundaries.

Free and open source

No price, no ads, no catch.

Ma is MIT licensed and built in the open. Every line is on GitHub, the builds run on GitHub Actions, and anyone can read exactly what the app does with their phone.
